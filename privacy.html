Privacy Policy — Tube+

Last Updated: October 26, 2025

Overview

Tube+ is a browser extension that enhances your YouTube experience with a mini overlay player and playback history tracking.

Data Collection and Storage

What Data We Collect

When history tracking is enabled, Tube+ stores:

  • YouTube video IDs (up to 20 videos)
  • Video titles
  • Timestamps of when videos were watched

Data Retention

History is limited to the last 20 videos. Videos are automatically deleted after 10 days. Combined limits ensure minimal data storage.

How Data is Stored

  • All data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's storage API.
  • No data is transmitted to external servers.
  • No personal information is collected (no names, emails, or browsing data beyond YouTube video history).
  • Data is not shared with any third parties.

User Control

  • History tracking is disabled by default.
  • Users must explicitly enable history tracking through the extension interface.
  • Users can clear their history at any time.
  • Users can disable history tracking at any time.

Permissions

Storage Permission

Used to save:

  • Your playback history (when enabled)
  • Overlay position preferences
  • Extension settings

Scripting Permission

Used to:

  • Inject the mini player overlay into web pages
  • Control YouTube video playback

ActiveTab Permission

Used to:

  • Access the current tab to inject the overlay when you press Alt+M
  • Communicate with YouTube tabs for playback control

Host Permissions (youtube.com)

Used to:

  • Monitor video changes on YouTube
  • Control video playback
  • Track watch history (when enabled)

Data Retention

History is limited to the last 20 videos. Videos are automatically deleted after 10 days. Data persists until you clear it, it expires, or you uninstall the extension. Uninstalling the extension removes all stored data immediately.

Children's Privacy

Tube+ does not knowingly collect any information from children. The extension does not collect personal information from any users.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date.

Summary

Tube+ respects your privacy:

  • ✅ All data stored locally on your device
  • ✅ No external servers or third-party sharing
  • ✅ History tracking disabled by default
  • ✅ Full user control over data
  • ✅ No personal information collected
